# Torii — three-minute demo

Built around the brief's own chain: **Audience discovery → Localisation & distribution → Monetisation & merch → Community retention.**

**Presenting slides?** Open `/pitch`: nine slides with live numbers from the app (← → to move, F full screen, Esc to leave). Each slide has a *Show it live* button that jumps to the matching screen. **No time for a live demo?** Play `public/demo-videos/torii-full-walkthrough.mp4` (about 6 minutes, captions burned in). It predates the pulse, passport and tags, so narrate those from `/pitch`.

Start from a fresh reset (home page → *Reset demo* → type `reset`) on the day you present: the Diwali poster's backers' day is the day after the reset. Times are cumulative.

**0:00 — The problem (home page).** "India loves Japanese shows; the money doesn't follow. Licensors can't see Indian demand, small deals cost too much to approve, and fans can't tell official from fake." Scroll to **From fandom to revenue, in four stages**: each card is a working part of Torii with a live number. "Everything here is fictional demo data."

**0:20 — 01 Audience discovery (`/pulse`, then Haruka → *Audience*).** On the pulse, point at the map: 600+ fans in 18 cities, answered before any campaign existed. Switch to Haruka (IP owner) → **Audience**. Walk the findings: Delhi/Bengaluru/Mumbai lead; **the Northeast has 40+ fans and nobody sells there**; the typical fan would pay ₹599 for a tee, so ₹699 reaches 44% of fans, not 65%; fans ask for stickers and hoodies the kit doesn't allow. Click **Summarise** for three next moves (Claude if a key is set, otherwise computed).

**0:55 — 02 Localisation & distribution (Haruka → *Asset kit*, Kabir, Riya).** The kit: approved artwork plus explicit rules (products, India only, price band, window, named approver). As Kabir, submit **Courier Chai Glass**: the server refuses it — *glassware isn't covered by this licence* — even though 26% of pulse fans want one. That's the owner's call, now backed by data. As Riya, open **Midnight Delivery Tee**: her Hinglish line, her 22 college clubs, her channels.

**1:30 — Approval (Haruka → *Overview → Open review* on Midnight v3).** Before/after proofs, what changed, re-run checks. **Approve version 3**: the seal carries the version fingerprint. Only the named approver can approve.

**1:50 — 03 Monetisation & merch (Aarav, fan).** On Midnight, preorder; the test-mode simulator confirms payment and the production gate is reached. Then Meera (operator) → *Operations*: release, ship, deliver. Open the **First Flight statement**: 100 units at ₹699 → ₹6,990 royalty, ₹5,592 creator, ₹8,388 Torii, all reconciled.

**2:20 — 04 Community retention (Aarav → *Passport*, then `/verify`).** Aarav's stamp book: three runs backed, one delivered. The vermilion banner: **Festival of Lanterns Poster is open to him first** — backers' day, because he backed an earlier run. (Signed out, the same page says "opens tomorrow".) Scroll to his **authenticity tag** and open it, or scan the QR with a phone on the public link: *Genuine — No. 005 of 100*, approved by Haruka, with the design fingerprint. Try a made-up code: *Not a Torii tag*, and the lookup is logged.

**Optional +0:40 — The fan app (Aarav, then Haruka).** As Aarav you land on **Read**: official manga in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and English. Scroll to **Official collabs, made in India**: the same campaigns, next to the series. Open **Sky Courier** (the demo series): its merch sits under the chapters. **Shelf** shows his manga and his collab preorders with their tag codes. Then Haruka → **Manga titles**: Kumo Lantern Studio's Partner Portal, where **Merch collabs** shows 3 collaborations, units backed and the ₹6,990 royalty from the same ledger.

**2:45 — Close (`/proof`).** "Nine claims, each with a target, measured live from the app's own records." Point at discovery, repeat backers (15%) and tags (100 of 100 units). "The ask: a 90-day pilot — one studio, three creators, one of them in the Northeast."
