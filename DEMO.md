# Torii — three-minute demo

**No time for a live demo?** Play `public/demo-videos/torii-full-walkthrough.mp4` (about 6 minutes, captions burned in), or present from the **How it works** page: 1–4 switch films, ← → step through chapters, F goes full screen.

Start from a fresh reset (home page → *Reset demo* → type `reset`). Times are cumulative.

**0:00 — The idea (home page).** "Torii is the gate between a licensed kit and a paid preorder." Point at the live tee framed in the gate, its production meter (45 of 50 paid) and its approval seal. Then point at the five stages, 一 to 五. "Everything you'll see today is fictional demo data."

**0:20 — 一 The kit (Haruka, IP owner → *Asset kit*).** Show the approved artwork and the explicit rules: T-shirt and poster only, India only, a price band, a licence window, and Haruka as the required approver. "Publishing a change creates a new kit version; old proposals stay tied to the rules they were made under."

**0:45 — 二 The creator offer (Kabir → *Courier Chai Glass*).** Click **Submit for approval**. The server refuses: *"Glassware isn't covered by this licence. Kit v2 permits T-shirt and Poster only."* Switch to Riya, open **Midnight Delivery Tee** and walk the tabs. Riya brings the Hinglish print line, her audience (22 college clubs), the distribution plan and the price. The preview is generated live from the design data. Optionally open **Brief assistant** and ask for two streetwear directions.

**1:20 — 三 Approval (Haruka → *Overview → Open review* on Midnight v3).** Show the before/after proofs, the "what changed" table (sizes, print line) and the re-run rule checks. "Fans are buying v2; approving v3 doesn't touch the live page until Riya publishes, and any later edit needs fresh approval." Click **Approve version 3**: the seal shows the version fingerprint. (Optional: as Daichi, Approve is disabled; only the named approver can approve.)

**1:50 — 四 Paid validation (Riya publishes v3 → Aarav, fan).** On the campaign page, choose XXL × 5 and preorder. The simulator is clearly marked test mode; click *Simulate successful payment*. On the order page, the production gate is reached. Optional: on the simulator, *Resend the same webhook event*, and it shows "duplicate ignored".

**2:20 — 五 Results (Meera, operator → *Operations*).**
- **Midnight:** *Release to production* → *Mark shipped* → *Mark delivered*, then *Replay all provider events*: "42 duplicates, ledger unchanged".
- **Monsoon Route Poster:** *Cancel and refund* refunds all 13 paid orders once. Running it again changes nothing.
- **First Flight statement:** ₹69,900 net item sales on 100 delivered units splits into ₹6,990 royalty, ₹5,592 creator and ₹8,388 Torii. Torii's share is revenue before expenses, not profit. Point out the reconciliation checks, the dashed "assumptions" panel kept apart from recorded figures, links to the underlying orders, and **Export CSV**.

**2:50 — Close.** "Rule checks enforce what the owner declared; people still make the creative and commercial calls. Payments are simulated behind an adapter that a real provider plugs into. What we want to learn with a partner is whether this makes small collaborations cheap enough to run."
