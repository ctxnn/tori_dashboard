# Size and Growth of the Japanese Anime/Manga Audience and Market in India

Research date: 2026-10-24. Reliability flags used throughout:
- **[SELF-REPORTED]**: a company or organizer stating its own numbers
- **[ANALYST EST.]**: a paid market-research forecast. Methods are opaque and estimates differ widely.
- **[SNIPPET]**: taken from a search-result summary because the page itself could not be fetched (403 or bot wall). Re-check before quoting on a slide.
- **[TRACKER]**: a box-office tracker figure (Sacnilk, Bollywood Hungama). Trackers disagree, and figures are estimates rather than distributor-audited numbers.

## 1. How many people in India watch anime? (audience size, streaming platforms)

### Takeaway
The best independent figure is GEM Partners' 2026 white paper. It puts India's anime viewing penetration at 41%, third in the world after Japan and China, with growth above 30% from 2020 to 2025. Crunchyroll itself claims about 180 million Indian anime fans (May 2024). No platform publishes a paid-subscriber count for India.

### Cited Findings
- **GEM Partners, Anime Global White Paper 2026 (released 16 April 2026):** India ranks **3rd globally in anime viewing penetration at 41%**, behind Japan (over 55%) and China (about 42%). India's penetration **grew more than 30% from 2020 to 2025**, and India and South Korea had the highest average audience growth rates. The survey covered **15,000 respondents across 15 countries** and measures penetration rates, not absolute audience size. — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/pop-culture/pop-culture-news/india-ranks-among-top-three-global-anime-viewership-markets)
- **Crunchyroll claims "~180 million Indian anime fans"**, per Akshat Sahu, Director of Marketing APAC at Crunchyroll, interviewed 20 May 2024. **[SELF-REPORTED]** The company has not published a methodology. — [Indian Retailer / Brand License](https://www.indianretailer.com/brandlicense/archives/interviews/anime-alchemy-crunchyrolls-epic-saga-india.i2912)
- **Crunchyroll India engagement (six months to May 2024):** minutes per viewer about **doubled (2x)** and total watch time rose **3.5x**. **[SELF-REPORTED]** — [Indian Retailer](https://www.indianretailer.com/brandlicense/archives/interviews/anime-alchemy-crunchyrolls-epic-saga-india.i2912)
- **Crunchyroll India catalog (May 2024):** 800+ anime titles, about 8,000 hours. The article gives 70+ dubbed shows in its introduction and 75+ in the interview, all in Hindi, Tamil and Telugu. **Dubbed content was over 65% of Crunchyroll's India viewership.** **[SELF-REPORTED]** — [Indian Retailer](https://www.indianretailer.com/brandlicense/archives/interviews/anime-alchemy-crunchyrolls-epic-saga-india.i2912)
- **Hindi dubs overtook English dubs** for *Jujutsu Kaisen* and *Chainsaw Man* in India, according to Crunchyroll's Akshat Sahu. No figures were given. **[SELF-REPORTED]** — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/pop-culture/pop-culture-news/india-ranks-among-top-three-global-anime-viewership-markets)
- **Netflix** is described as the dominant anime platform in India. No share figure is given, so treat this as weak. Netflix India began systematic **Hindi anime dubbing in 2020**, starting with Studio Ghibli titles. Crunchyroll added **regional-language dubs for most of its library in 2023**. — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/pop-culture/pop-culture-news/india-ranks-among-top-three-global-anime-viewership-markets)
- **Crunchyroll global paid subscribers**, for context: **13 million** (cited May 2024), per [Indian Retailer](https://www.indianretailer.com/brandlicense/archives/interviews/anime-alchemy-crunchyrolls-epic-saga-india.i2912). Above **17 million** in 2025, per [Variety](https://variety.com/2025/film/global/anime-crunchyroll-annecy-slate-demon-slayer-1236429179). An earlier target of 25 million by the end of 2025 looked unlikely to be met, per [Popverse](https://www.thepopverse.com/tv-crunchyroll-anime-subscribers-unlikely-to-hit-target-25-million-2025-why). No India split is published.
- **Indian SVOD context:** Media Partners Asia projects **193 million direct-to-consumer SVOD subscribers in India by 2026**, and SVOD revenue rising from **$800M (2021) to over $1.8B (2026)**. FICCI-PwC expected regional-language OTT viewing to pass 50% of time spent by 2025. Both are cited secondhand. **[ANALYST EST.]** — [Indian Retailer](https://www.indianretailer.com/brandlicense/archives/interviews/anime-alchemy-crunchyrolls-epic-saga-india.i2912)
- **Crunchyroll's India investment:** a second Indian office in **Hyderabad**, and a "Wish Your World Was Anime" campaign with Bollywood and Telugu stars **Rashmika Mandanna and Tiger Shroff**. — [Indian Retailer](https://www.indianretailer.com/brandlicense/archives/interviews/anime-alchemy-crunchyrolls-epic-saga-india.i2912)
- **Unverified claim, do not use:** a LinkedIn post says South India anime viewing grows 40–45% a year and that tier-2/3 youth are over 60% of new anime users. It is a personal post with no methodology. — [LinkedIn](https://in.linkedin.com/in/b-pramod)

### Inferences
- Two different estimates point the same way. One is an independent penetration survey (41%, third globally). The other is a large self-reported fan count (180M). The 180M figure is roughly 12–13% of India's population, which looks plausible next to the 41% figure, assuming GEM's sample was the online, urban population. Pitch the 180M as "Crunchyroll's estimate", not as a measured figure.
- Dubs account for over 65% of viewing, and Hindi now beats English on major titles. That suggests growth is coming from a mass audience that is not English-first, beyond the metro core of fans. This matters for revenue design: regional-language merchandise, pricing and commerce.
- The gap between about 180M claimed fans and no published paid-subscriber base supports the JETRO problem statement directly. The fandom is large, but monetization is undisclosed and probably thin.

### Gaps
- No Crunchyroll India paid-subscriber or MAU figure was found. Crunchyroll does not disclose India numbers.
- No Netflix India anime viewership share or hours were found. Netflix's global "What We Watched" reports were not checked for India-specific anime data.
- Ormax Media, Kantar and YouGov India anime audience sizing were not found in this pass.
- The history of Animax India and Sony YAY was not found: no data on Sony YAY anime programming, Animax India's launch and closure dates, or reach.
- Anime on JioCinema/JioHotstar and Prime Video India was not covered: no viewership numbers found.

## 2. What share of Indian Gen Z / 18–24 watch anime?

### Takeaway
No India-only Gen Z percentage was found. The closest data is the Crunchyroll-commissioned NRG survey (2025), which included India in its seven markets. It found **54% of Gen Z identify as anime fans**, ahead of K-dramas (43%) and Bollywood (39%) in that age group. These are figures for the whole sample, not India alone.

### Cited Findings
- **Crunchyroll x National Research Group, "How Anime Became a Worldwide Cultural Force" (May 2025):** nearly **29,000 respondents aged 13–54 in 7 markets, including India**, with about 3,500 engaged anime fans. **54% of Gen Z identify as anime fans.** **44% of all entertainment consumers aged 13–54 identify as anime fans**, more than identify with K-dramas or Bollywood. **[SELF-REPORTED: Crunchyroll-commissioned]** — [Anime News Network](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/interest/2025-05-23/crunchyroll-research-over-half-of-gen-z-globally-are-anime-fans/.224732); [AWN](https://www.awn.com/news/new-crunchyroll-commissioned-study-reveals-animes-impact-gen-z); [Kidscreen](https://kidscreen.com/2025/05/22/report-anime-soars-in-popularity-with-teens/)
- **In the same study, Gen Z anime fandom (54%) beat K-dramas (43%) and Bollywood (39%).** This is a sample-wide comparison, not India-only. **[SNIPPET]** — [Outlook Respawn (R. Madhavan article)](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/pop-culture/pop-culture-news/r-madhavan-warns-indian-cinema-is-losing-gen-z-to-anime)
- **Qualitative signal:** Outlook Respawn ran the headline "R. Madhavan warns Indian cinema is losing Gen Z to anime." An Indian film star publicly framed anime as competition for young audiences. — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/pop-culture/pop-culture-news/r-madhavan-warns-indian-cinema-is-losing-gen-z-to-anime)
- **Follow-up NRG study (reported 9 Oct 2026):** about **5,000 anime viewers across 10 markets including India**, plus 500 in Japan. **79% of fans aged 13–17 and 68% of Gen Z fans say they watch more anime than a year ago.** These are sample-wide figures. **[SNIPPET]** **[SELF-REPORTED]** — [Japan Times](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/culture/2026/10/09/entertainment-news/anime-survey-watching/)
- Sources disagree on definitions. Some describe the 2025 sample as 13–54 and others as 18–54, and Gen Z is defined as ages 13–28 in some coverage. — [Anime News Network](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/interest/2025-05-23/crunchyroll-research-over-half-of-gen-z-globally-are-anime-fans/.224732)

### Inferences
- India was one of seven survey markets, and GEM Partners ranks India third globally in penetration. So India's Gen Z figure is likely at or above the 54% sample-wide average, but this is inference, not data. On a slide: "54% of Gen Z across 7 markets incl. India (Crunchyroll/NRG 2025)". Do not write "54% of Indian Gen Z".
- Anime beating Bollywood among Gen Z in a survey that includes India is a strong pitch line, but it must be attributed to a Crunchyroll-commissioned study.

### Gaps
- No India-only Gen Z or 18–24 anime share from Crunchyroll/NRG. The full report may hold country breakdowns, but none were found in press coverage.
- No YouGov India, Ormax, Kantar, Statista or Toei India survey on anime use by age was found.
- The Japan Times page could not be fetched (403), so India-specific numbers from the 2026 study, if any, are unknown.

## 3. India anime market revenue size and projected CAGR (all [ANALYST EST.])

### Takeaway
Analyst estimates of India's anime market range from about **USD 1.1 billion (2024, IMARC)** to **USD 2.1 billion (2025, Ken Research)**, with forecast growth of **11.5–13.3% a year**. Manga estimates are inconsistent: IMARC's figure is about 20 times Grand View's. Anime merchandise alone is estimated at **USD 256M, rising to USD 528M by 2030** (Grand View). Use ranges, and lead with the conservative figure.

### Cited Findings
- **IMARC, India anime market:** **USD 1,098 million in 2024**, rising to **USD 2,930 million by 2033**, a **CAGR of 11.5% (2025–2033)**. **[ANALYST EST.]** — [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/india-anime-market)
- **Ken Research, India anime market:** **USD 2,107 million in 2025**, forecast **CAGR of 13.26%** (2026–2031). **[ANALYST EST.]** — [Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/industry-reports/india-anime-market.md)
- **Grand View Research, India anime market:** **13% CAGR from 2024 to 2030**, cited secondhand. **[ANALYST EST.]** — [Indian Retailer](https://www.indianretailer.com/brandlicense/archives/interviews/anime-alchemy-crunchyrolls-epic-saga-india.i2912)
- **IMARC, India manga market:** **USD 615.9 million in 2025**, rising to **USD 3,549.3 million by 2034**, a **CAGR of 20.84% (2026–2034)**. **[ANALYST EST.]** — [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/india-manga-market)
- **Grand View Research, India manga market:** **USD 28.3 million in 2025**, **CAGR of 22.3% (2026–2033)**, with digital the largest content type in 2025. **[ANALYST EST.]** — [Grand View Research Horizon](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/horizon/outlook/manga-market/india)
- **Grand View Research, India comic book market:** **USD 762.7 million (2025)**, with manga within it growing at a **13.0% CAGR from 2025 to 2030**. Hard-copy comics were **79.9% of revenue in 2024**. **[ANALYST EST.]** — [Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/india-comic-book-market-report); [Yahoo Finance / ResearchAndMarkets](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/india-comic-book-market-size-152500060.html)
- **Grand View Research, India anime merchandise:** **USD 256 million**, projected to reach **USD 528 million by 2030**. Outlook Respawn itself warns that such projections should be "viewed with caution". **[ANALYST EST.]** — [Outlook Respawn (Aug 2025)](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/amp/story/pop-culture/pop-culture-originals/inside-indias-booming-anime-merchandise-trade)
- **Merchandise price friction:** licensed figures in India often cost **40–45% more than Japanese retail** because of import duties. A $40 figure can sell for **₹5,000 or more**. Counterfeits are called a "persistent problem", with no share given. Named sellers include Souled Store, Comicsense, The Entertainment Store, Anime Devta (a homegrown brand), F-Bloc (Kolkata), Uniqlo, Celio, Flipkart, Amazon India and Myntra. — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/amp/story/pop-culture/pop-culture-originals/inside-indias-booming-anime-merchandise-trade)
- **Bandai Namco India** is reportedly preparing **made-in-India anime apparel** (One Piece, Dragon Ball, Naruto) with India-exclusive designs and more retail space. This is based on unnamed sources, with no official announcement found, May 2026. It currently imports figurines and Gunpla through partners such as Fat Cat Collectibles and Wizplex. — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/pop-culture/pop-culture-news/bandai-namco-made-in-india-anime-merchandise); background interview with COO Takeo Yagi (2023): [Animehunch](https://animehunch.com/bandai-namco-india-says-we-are-going-to-provide-products-for-all-anime-fans/)
- **Regional context:** Asia-Pacific held **49.3% of the global anime market in 2025** (MMR Statistics, cited secondhand). **[ANALYST EST.]** — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/pop-culture/pop-culture-news/india-ranks-among-top-three-global-anime-viewership-markets)

### Inferences
- Every analyst estimate shows double-digit growth: 11.5–13.3% for anime and 13–22% for manga. The direction is consistent even though the absolute sizes are not.
- The manga estimates (IMARC $616M vs Grand View $28M) are too far apart to quote without comment. They probably define "manga" differently, for example IMARC may include anime-adjacent digital comics and webtoons. For a pitch, cite the anime market (about $1.1B in 2024, 11.5% CAGR, IMARC) as the conservative headline and present manga as "fast-growing, 20%+ CAGR by multiple analysts".
- Import duties (40–45% markups) and counterfeits are structural barriers to official merchandise revenue. Bandai Namco's reported move to local manufacturing shows a Japanese licensor responding to that. This fits the JETRO "sustainable revenue" angle: local production plus licensing.

### Gaps
- No primary, audited figure exists for anime revenue in India. All figures are paywalled analyst reports reached through their summary pages.
- Mordor Intelligence was not found for India-specific anime.
- No revenue figures were found for any India anime merchandise retailer (Souled Store, Redwolf and others).

## 5. Crunchyroll India pricing in INR and India-specific moves

### Takeaway
In July 2022 Crunchyroll moved India to rupee pricing at **₹79/month (Fan) and ₹99/month (Mega Fan)**, among its cheapest prices worldwide. It has invested heavily in **Hindi, Tamil and Telugu dubs** (75+ shows by 2024, over 65% of India viewing) and opened a **Hyderabad office**. No evidence of UPI-specific moves or a Sony YAY tie-in was found.

## 7. Manga sales in India

### Takeaway
Manga is clearly growing but is poorly measured. A Deloitte India partner cites a **300–400% rise in manga and anime content since Covid** and **300,000–500,000 manga copies in circulation**, both from a page seen only as a search snippet. Analyst manga market sizes range from **USD 28M to USD 616M**, so they are unreliable as absolute figures.

### Cited Findings
- **Business Standard (14 Jan 2025), "India emerges as Japanese Manga's latest 'superhero'":** Deloitte India's **Chandrashekhar Mantha** describes a **300–400% increase in manga and anime content since Covid** and estimates **300,000–500,000 copies in circulation** in the books segment. A **Penguin Random House India** executive credits physical bookstore growth and online platforms such as **Amazon** for wider access. **[SNIPPET]**: the page returned 403 when fetched. — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/entertainment/india-emerges-as-japanese-manga-s-latest-superhero-amid-rising-interest-125011400859_1.html)
- **Analyst manga estimates:** IMARC puts it at USD 615.9M (2025) with a 20.84% CAGR to USD 3,549.3M by 2034 ([IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/india-manga-market)). Grand View puts it at USD 28.3M (2025) with a 22.3% CAGR, digital-led ([Grand View Horizon](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/horizon/outlook/manga-market/india)). Grand View's comic-book report has manga growing at 13% CAGR from 2025 to 2030, with hard copy at 79.9% of 2024 comic revenue ([Grand View](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/india-comic-book-market-report)). **[ANALYST EST.]**, and the estimates conflict, including on print versus digital.
- **Retail history:** Landmark and Crossword began importing manga and anime merchandise in the mid-2000s. Anime first reached Indian TV in the late 1990s (Pokémon, Beyblade, Dragon Ball Z). — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/amp/story/pop-culture/pop-culture-originals/inside-indias-booming-anime-merchandise-trade)
- **Bookstores and Amazon:** a Cambridge University Press study of Indian bookstores notes the **temporary weakening of Amazon's near-monopoly** during the pandemic and bookstores moving onto online channels. This is context only, not manga-specific. — [Cambridge Elements](https://www.cambridge.org/core/elements/brickandmortar-bookstore-in-contemporary-india/FB06323DBEFCB07C20A72A2AB77C9D7D)

### Inferences
- The direction is consistent: every source says manga is growing fast, with 13–22% CAGRs and a 300–400% rise since Covid. But 300k–500k copies in circulation is small next to 180M claimed anime fans. Manga is under-penetrated relative to anime viewing, which suggests unmet demand. Likely barriers are import pricing, distribution and the lack of Hindi or regional-language manga.
- Analysts disagree on print versus digital (Grand View's manga report says digital leads, its comics report says print does). That points to a fragmented market with no authoritative sales tracking, which a pitch could turn into an opportunity: data and direct-to-fan commerce.

### Gaps
- No Amazon India bestseller or category growth data for manga was found.
- No figures were found on Penguin Random House India, Viz, Kodansha or Shueisha distribution volumes, or on any India-localized manga editions or translations.
- No India user numbers were found for Manga Plus (Shueisha) or other legal digital manga apps.
- No data was found on piracy or scanlation rates in India.
